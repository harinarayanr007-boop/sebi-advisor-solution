The Diagnosis

The ₹50 Lakh HNI Ghosting Trap

Most Indian SEBI RIAs are world-class financial planners, but their onboarding experience causes 60% of qualified leads to abandon before signing the advisory agreement.

1. The "Free Advice" Illusion Barrier
When an HNI lands on your website, they subconsciously compare your ₹50,000–₹85,000 flat fee with bank relationship managers who offer "free" wealth management. Without an instant mathematical tool demonstrating the 1.5% hidden commission drag of regular mutual funds, prospects bounce.
2. The 5-Day Manual Friction Wall
After an initial inquiry, sending a 10-page Word document or static PDF for risk profiling creates immediate cognitive fatigue. Busy founders, doctors, and CXOs postpone filling it out, leading to severe drop-offs before the discovery call ever happens.
3. The Compounding Revenue Loss
Every lost HNI prospect isn't just a lost ₹50,000 annual fee—it is a lost 5-year lifetime value of ₹2,50,000 to ₹4,50,000 plus lost HNI referrals. Planners don't have a lead problem; they have an onboarding friction problem.

PAGE 04 • RETENTION ANCHORS
Maximizing LTV

The Year-2 Retention Anchor

Why do clients churn after 12 months? Because when markets rise, they credit luck; when markets fall, they blame the fee. Here is how onboarding sets up 90%+ annual renewals:

1. Anchor as a Fiduciary Coach, Not a Stock Picker
Your onboarding portal must clearly communicate your direct-plan, evidence-based asset allocation ethos. When clients understand that your job is behavioral risk management and direct-cost elimination, renewal is never tied to short-term market fluctuations.
2. Transparent Milestones Defined Upfront
Set crystal-clear quarterly deliverables inside the onboarding flow: (Q1) Risk & Asset Allocation Plan, (Q2) Insurance & Estate Review, (Q3) Tax-Loss Harvesting & Direct Rebalancing, (Q4) Annual Progress Audit. Clients renew when they see an ongoing roadmap.
3. The Ongoing "Fee Drag Offset" Metric
Every annual review report reminds the client: "By using direct mutual funds through our advisory, you saved ₹1,42,000 in trail commissions this year, offsetting our ₹65,000 fee by more than double." The fee pays for itself.
